The impact files retain their source number because their pitch order is used by
the combat feedback mapping. No attribution is required by CC0; this notice is
kept so the source of the project assets remains easy to trace.

Older Kenney impact files in this folder are retained only for rollback and are
not referenced by the current combat-audio code.
